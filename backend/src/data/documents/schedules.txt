Global English Academy offers flexible schedules for adult learners:
1. Intensive Weekday Program: Monday to Thursday from 7:00 PM to 9:00 PM (8 hours/week).
2. Saturday Immersion Program: Saturdays from 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM (5 hours/week).
3. Morning Booster: Tuesday and Thursday from 6:30 AM to 8:00 AM (3 hours/week).
All classes are delivered via live interactive virtual sessions led by certified native or bilingual instructors. Classes start on the first Monday of every calendar month.
