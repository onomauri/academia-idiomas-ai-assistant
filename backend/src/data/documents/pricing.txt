Tuition and Pricing Policies for Global English Academy:
1. Standard Monthly Plan: $120 USD per month. Includes full access to live classes, digital workbook, and weekly speaking clubs.
2. Semester Full Payment Plan: $600 USD (one single payment covering 6 consecutive months, granting a 16% discount).
3. Registration Fee: A one-time registration and placement fee of $25 USD applies to new students.
Payment methods accepted: Credit/Debit Cards, PayPal, and Colombian local transfers (PSE, Bancolombia). Refunds are eligible only within the first 5 business days after enrollment.
